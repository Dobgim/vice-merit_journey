The **Leiden Excellence Scholarship 2027** (LExS) is one of the most useful tuition awards in the Netherlands for international master's students, and the **deadline is 1 December 2026**. If you want to start a master's at Leiden in September 2027 and you come from outside the EEA, you have less than two weeks to get your application and your short motivation letter ready.

This guide covers who can apply, what the scholarship is worth, and how to put together a strong application in the time you have left. You can see the key facts on our [Leiden Excellence Scholarship listing](https://www.meritleaders.com/scholarships/leiden-university-excellence-scholarship-lexs-2027).

## What is the Leiden Excellence Scholarship?

LExS is a merit-based award from Leiden University for outstanding students from outside the European Economic Area who are starting a master's programme. It is **not a full scholarship**. It reduces your tuition fee, so you will still need to budget for living costs and show proof of funds for your visa or residence permit.

Only a small number of awards are made each year, so competition is real. That is exactly why the quality of your motivation letter matters.

## How much is the award worth?

The award is a contribution towards your tuition, with several levels depending on the selection. Our listing summarises the range, and the university's scholarship page gives the current levels for your intake. Because amounts can differ between programmes and years, always check the official page linked from our listing before you plan your budget.

## Who can apply?

You are likely eligible if you:

- hold a **non-EEA / non-EFTA passport** and are not entitled to Dutch student finance;
- are applying for a **master's programme at Leiden** starting in September 2027;
- have **excellent academic results** that fit the master's you are applying for. Being among the top 10% of your graduating class is the rough benchmark, not a hard rule.

A few groups are excluded. You cannot apply if you already hold a Leiden master's degree, and students on the LLM (non-advanced) and MSc programmes at Leiden Law School are not eligible. The LLM Advanced Studies programmes and the MSc in International Relations and Diplomacy are treated differently and are open to all nationalities.

## When is the Leiden Excellence Scholarship 2027 deadline?

The deadline is **1 December 2026**. Convert this to your own time zone, and aim to finish a few days early. Late applications are not considered, and once you submit, your application cannot be changed.

Decisions are normally communicated before the end of March 2027.

## How do you apply for LExS?

You apply for admission and the scholarship at the same time.

1. **Apply for admission** to your master's programme through Leiden's online application system, and pay the application fee if one applies to your programme.
2. In the **scholarships section** of the application, indicate that you want to be considered for the LExS.
3. **Upload your motivation letter** as a PDF of no more than **500 words**.
4. Submit everything before 1 December 2026.

Creating and submitting the admission application can take a few working days, particularly if you are waiting on transcripts, so do not leave it to the last evening.

## How do you write the 500-word motivation letter?

Five hundred words is short, which makes every sentence count. Panels want to see why *you* deserve one of a limited number of awards, not a general essay about education.

### What should you include?

- **Why this programme at Leiden.** Name specific courses, research groups or staff that fit your goals.
- **Your academic strengths.** Mention results, projects, a thesis or research experience that show you can excel.
- **Your plan after graduating.** Be concrete about the work you want to do and how the master's gets you there.
- **Why the scholarship matters to you.** Be honest and brief. Do not exaggerate hardship.

### What should you avoid?

- Copying a generic letter you have sent to other universities.
- Repeating your CV line by line.
- Vague claims such as "I am passionate about learning" with no evidence.
- Going over the word limit.

Write it yourself, in your own voice, and ask a teacher or adviser for feedback. If you want a second pair of eyes, our advisers review and coach, so the final letter stays yours. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is the Leiden Excellence Scholarship fully funded?

No. It is a tuition award, so you need to plan separately for living costs and show proof of sufficient funds for your visa.

### Can I apply if I have not yet been admitted?

Yes. You apply for admission and the scholarship together in the same online application.

### Can I apply after 1 December?

No. Late applications are not considered, and applications cannot be edited after submission.

### Is there a fee to apply for the scholarship?

The scholarship request itself is free. Any admission application fee depends on your programme. Never pay anyone who promises to "guarantee" a scholarship.
