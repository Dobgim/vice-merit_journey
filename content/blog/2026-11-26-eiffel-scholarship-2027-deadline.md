The **Eiffel Scholarship 2027 deadline** is **6 January 2027**, but that date is not really yours. The France Excellence Eiffel Scholarship is the French government's flagship award for international master's and PhD students, and you cannot apply for it yourself. A French university or school has to nominate you, and each institution sets its own, earlier internal deadline, often in November or December.

That is why the next few weeks matter. This guide explains how the scholarship works, who it suits and what to do now. You can see the key facts on our [France Excellence Eiffel Scholarship 2027 listing](https://www.meritleaders.com/scholarships/france-excellence-eiffel-scholarship-2027).

## What is the Eiffel Scholarship?

It is a scholarship run by Campus France on behalf of the French Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs. It supports talented international students who want to take a master's or a PhD (or a joint-supervision PhD) at a French institution, and it is open to all nationalities.

The aim is to attract future leaders in fields such as engineering, economics, management, law and political science. Competition is real, so a strong, well-prepared file is what makes the difference.

## What does the Eiffel Scholarship pay?

The scholarship pays a monthly allowance for living costs, currently **€1,200 a month at master's level and €2,000 a month at PhD level**. On top of that it covers help with international travel and health insurance, among other services. It does not replace your tuition fees in the way some scholarships do, so check what your chosen programme charges.

Because amounts are reviewed each year, always read the current call on the official page, which you can reach through our listing.

## Who can apply?

You must be an international student, meaning you are not a French resident, and you must be nominated by a French institution that has admitted you or is considering your admission. A few other points to keep in mind:

- There are age limits at master's and PhD level. Check the current rules in the official call before you invest time.
- You need a real academic project and a clear reason for choosing France.
- Applying directly to Campus France makes you ineligible.

## How do I apply for the Eiffel Scholarship?

The process runs the opposite way from most scholarships. Here is the order that works.

1. **Choose your programmes.** Pick two or three French master's or doctoral programmes that match your background. Strong, research-oriented programmes at well-regarded institutions tend to be the best nominators.
2. **Apply for admission.** Submit the university's own application and follow its deadline. This often comes first.
3. **Contact the international office.** Ask whether the institution nominates candidates for the Eiffel programme and what its internal deadline is. Do this immediately.
4. **Prepare your scholarship file.** Your university will ask for your CV, transcripts, a motivation letter and sometimes references.
5. **Let your institution submit.** The university uploads your file to Campus France. You do not.
6. **Wait for the result.** Winners are told in the week of 12 April 2027.

## When is the deadline?

Campus France gives **6 January 2027** as the date by which institutions must submit files. Your own deadline is earlier. Ask each university for its internal date, and aim to be ready two weeks before it. Convert any time given to your own time zone.

If you are still choosing universities, start with your shortlist this week. Delays at this stage are the most common reason strong candidates miss out.

## What makes an Eiffel application strong?

Selectors look for academic excellence, a clear project and a link to France. A few habits help:

- **Show results.** Strong grades and a ranking within your class carry weight.
- **Explain your project.** Say what you want to study, why in France and how it connects to your future plans.
- **Be specific.** Name courses, professors or labs that attract you.
- **Tell your story honestly.** Your own voice matters, and every claim should be one you can back up.
