The **Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme 2027** (HKPFS) is one of the best-funded PhD awards in Asia, and it is open to students of every nationality and every field. The **initial application deadline is 1 December 2026**, so if you want to start a PhD in Hong Kong in 2027/28, you have about two weeks to get moving.

This guide explains what the fellowship pays, who can apply, how the two-step process works and how to put together an application that stands out. You can see the key facts on our [Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme listing](https://www.meritleaders.com/scholarships/hong-kong-phd-fellowship-scheme).

## What is the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme?

HKPFS is run by Hong Kong's Research Grants Council to attract outstanding PhD students from around the world. It is for new, full-time PhD students at the eight publicly funded universities in Hong Kong:

- The University of Hong Kong
- The Chinese University of Hong Kong
- The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology
- The Hong Kong Polytechnic University
- City University of Hong Kong
- Hong Kong Baptist University
- Lingnan University
- The Education University of Hong Kong

A total of **400 fellowships** are awarded for the 2027/28 academic year. Nationality, work experience and background do not count against you.

## How much does the HKPFS pay?

Each fellow receives:

- an **annual stipend of HK$344,400** (roughly US$44,000), and
- a **conference and research travel allowance of HK$14,400 per year**,

for up to **three years**. That is enough to cover living costs in Hong Kong while you focus on your research. Check the university's own pages for any housing arrangements, which vary by institution.

## Who can apply?

You can apply if you are seeking admission as a **new full-time PhD student** at one of the eight universities above. There is no nationality restriction. You must also meet the admission requirements of the programme you choose, and these differ between universities and departments, so read them carefully before you start.

Strong candidates usually show:

- excellent academic results (this is the main selection factor);
- real research ability and potential, shown through a thesis, publications, projects or lab experience;
- good communication skills and signs of leadership.

## When is the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme 2027 deadline?

There are two deadlines, and both fall on **1 December 2026**:

1. **The initial application** on the RGC's online system closes at **12:00 noon Hong Kong time**.
2. **Your application to each university** closes at **11:59 pm Hong Kong time**.

Convert both to your own time zone, and note that the initial application closes earlier in the day. Late submissions are not considered, so submit as early as you can.

Results are announced by May 2027.

## How do you apply for the HKPFS 2027/28?

1. **Choose your programme(s).** You can name up to two choices of university, department or programme, in priority order. Pick programmes where a specific supervisor works on your topic.
2. **Submit the initial application online** to the Research Grants Council before the noon deadline.
3. **Apply to your chosen universities** through their own PhD admission systems, with all the documents they ask for, before the university deadline.
4. **Wait for nomination.** Universities nominate candidates, and the fellowship panels make the final awards. Your fellowship depends on being nominated by the university.

Because the fellowship is attached to admission, a weak or incomplete university application cannot be rescued by a strong initial form. Treat both steps as one project.

## How do you make your application stronger?

### Contact supervisors early

Read recent papers from the department, then send a short, polite email explaining your interests and asking whether they are taking PhD students. Supervisors who know your work can make a real difference when nominations are decided.

### Write a clear research statement

Explain the question you want to work on, what you have already done, and why this department is the right place. Specific beats impressive-sounding. Our guide to the [statement of purpose](https://www.meritleaders.com/blog/statement-of-purpose-scholarship) covers structure and common mistakes, and the same principles apply to a research statement.

### Prepare your referees now

Ask your referees this week, give them your CV and a short summary of your plans, and tell them the deadline. Our advice on [recommendation letters](https://www.meritleaders.com/blog/scholarship-recommendation-letter) shows how to make it easy for them to write something specific and strong.

### Get your documents ready

Transcripts, degree certificates, English-language evidence if your programme requires it, and a CV should all be ready before you open the forms. Certified copies and translations can take days.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I apply to more than one university?

You can list up to two programme choices, which may be at one or two universities. Use the second choice wisely.

### Is the fellowship only for science subjects?

No. It covers all fields. Applications are assessed by one panel for sciences, medicine, engineering and technology, and another for humanities, social sciences and business.

### Do I need to pay an application fee?

Fees are set by each university, so check your chosen programme's page. Never pay anyone who promises a guaranteed fellowship; genuine applications go only through the official RGC and university systems.
