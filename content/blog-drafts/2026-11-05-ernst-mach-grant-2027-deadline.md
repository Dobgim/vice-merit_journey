Do you want to spend a few months doing research at an Austrian university? The **Ernst Mach Grant 2027/28 deadline** is **1 December 2026**, so the time to start is now. The grant is run by the OeAD, Austria's agency for education and internationalisation, for the Austrian Government. It supports researchers from outside Austria, and that includes students and early-career academics from Cameroon and the rest of Africa.

This guide explains who can apply, what the grant looks like, which documents take the longest to get, and how to use the weeks you have left.

## What is the Ernst Mach Grant Worldwide?

The Ernst Mach Grant Worldwide supports **research or study stays in Austria of 1 to 9 months**. It is open to all fields of study. It is not a full degree scholarship. It funds a visit to an Austrian university, usually to work with a supervisor on your PhD or postdoctoral research.

On our site it is listed as a partially funded grant. You get a monthly grant for the length of your stay. We could not confirm the 2027/28 monthly amount, so please **check the official OeAD page** for the current rate before you plan your budget.

Stays for this call start from September 2027. The OeAD says they cannot be extended, so choose your stay length carefully.

## Who can apply for the Ernst Mach Grant?

According to the OeAD's FAQ for this call, the grant is for people from universities outside Austria. You can apply if you are:

- a **doctoral (PhD) student** at a university outside Austria;
- a **postgraduate or post-doc** who finished your studies abroad after 30 September 2025 and wants to do research in Austria; or
- a **post-doc working as a lecturer or researcher** at a foreign university.

There is an age limit of **35**. You cannot apply if you are a regular student at an Austrian university, if you live permanently in Austria, or if you did most of your academic education in Austria.

If you are a Cameroonian PhD student, a young lecturer, or a recent master's graduate with a clear research plan, this call is worth a serious look. If you are still in your first degree, it is not the right fit. Our [eligibility checker](https://www.meritleaders.com/eligibility) can point you to better options.

## When is the deadline?

Applications close at **23:59 CET on 1 December 2026**. Late applications are a common reason strong candidates miss out, and Austrian offices are unlikely to bend the rule. Aim to submit at least a few days early.

Results are expected in early July 2027, so you need patience. The stay itself begins in the 2027/28 academic year.

## What documents do I need?

The biggest delay for most applicants is not the online form. It is getting letters signed and stamped. Based on the OeAD's FAQ, plan for the following:

- **A supervision confirmation from an Austrian university lecturer.** You must use the OeAD template. It needs a date, signature and stamp. The supervisor must also confirm your project can be done in English.
- **Recommendation letters.** They can be freely worded but need letterhead, a date, a signature and the institution's stamp. They must be no older than six months at the time you apply.
- **A valid biometric passport.** An ID card is only accepted for EU applicants, so for Cameroonian applicants the passport is the document to have ready.
- **Translations.** Documents in English need no German translation. Documents in other languages need a copy of the original plus an English or German translation.

You do not need an official English test such as TOEFL. You do need enough English for your subject area.

There is **no separate research proposal upload**. Instead, evaluators judge your plan from what you write in the online application, so every section counts. The OeAD also asks you to **declare any use of generative AI**, including which tools you used and how. Write your own answers and be open about any help you had.

## How do I find an Austrian supervisor?

This is the step that decides most applications. Without a supervisor who agrees to host you, you cannot apply.

1. Search the websites of Austrian universities for researchers working on your topic.
2. Read two or three of their recent papers so you can talk about them sincerely.
3. Send a short, polite email: who you are, your research question, why you chose them, and the months you would like to visit.
4. Follow up once after a week. Academics are busy, and a calm reminder is normal.
5. When they agree, send the OeAD template early so they have time to sign and stamp it.

Do this **this week**. Waiting until late November is risky because supervisors may be travelling or slow to reply.

## Application checklist

- [ ] Confirm you meet the eligibility rules and are 35 or younger
- [ ] Shortlist 3 to 5 Austrian researchers and email them
- [ ] Get the supervisor's letter on the OeAD template, signed and stamped
- [ ] Ask two referees for letters on letterhead, dated within six months
- [ ] Check your biometric passport is valid
- [ ] Prepare translations for documents not in English or German
- [ ] Complete every section of the online form in your own words
- [ ] Declare any generative AI use
- [ ] Submit before 1 December 2026, 23:59 CET

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Starting with the form, not the supervisor.** Contact supervisors first.
- **Old or unstamped letters.** A letter older than six months can be rejected.
- **Vague plans.** State what you will do each month and what you will produce.
- **Asking for a stay that is too long or too short.** Match the months to your actual work plan.
- **Paying anyone for a "guaranteed" grant.** The OeAD application is free. If someone promises a place for a fee, walk away.
