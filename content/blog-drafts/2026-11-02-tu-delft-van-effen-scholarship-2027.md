If you want a funded engineering or technology master's in Europe, the **TU Delft Van Effen scholarship 2027** deserves a place on your shortlist. The deadline is **1 December 2026 at 23:59 CET**, so there is very little time left. This guide explains who can apply, what the award covers and what to do this month.

The full name is the **Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarship**. It is offered by Delft University of Technology in the Netherlands to excellent international students starting a two-year MSc.

You can see our listing here: [TU Delft Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarship 2027](https://www.meritleaders.com/scholarships/tu-delft-justus-louise-van-effen-excellence-scholarship-2027). The official details are on the [TU Delft scholarship page](https://www.tudelft.nl/onderwijs/studievoorlichting/praktische-zaken/scholarships/justus-louise-van-effen-excellence-scholarships).

## When is the TU Delft Van Effen scholarship 2027 deadline?

The scholarship application deadline is **1 December 2026, 23:59 CET**. Successful candidates are told by email by the end of March 2027.

Be careful here: the MSc programme itself may have a later deadline for students with a non-Dutch bachelor's degree (for some programmes this is 1 April 2027). **That later date does not apply if you want the scholarship.** You must submit your complete MSc application before 1 December.

## What does the scholarship cover?

The award covers your full tuition fees plus a contribution to living costs for the two years of the MSc. TU Delft's own pages give about **€60,000 for non-EU students**, but other TU Delft publications quote different figures, and amounts can change by year. Please **check the official page** for the current amount before you plan your budget.

At least 16 scholarships are available every year, with two per faculty. That is a small number, so competition is strong.

## Who can apply?

Based on the official page, you should:

- Be an international applicant, conditionally admitted to a regular two-year TU Delft MSc programme (joint programmes are not included).
- Show academic excellence. As a guide, you should be among the **top 10% of graduates** in your previous study programme. Proof of ranking is not required.
- Not be a current TU Delft bachelor's student.
- Not hold a bachelor's degree from a Dutch university while applying as an international student.
- Not have already started your MSc at TU Delft.

Cameroonian and other African students can apply, as long as they meet these conditions. Always read the latest rules on the official page, as they are the final word.

## What do I need to submit?

Everything must reach TU Delft together, before the deadline:

1. A complete application to your chosen MSc programme.
2. The scholarship application form.
3. A reference letter (the official page lists the reference requirements, so read them carefully).

An incomplete file is the most avoidable way to lose a place. Do not leave the references until the last week.

## How do I apply step by step?

### 1. Pick the right MSc programme

Read the admission requirements for your programme first. Your bachelor's subject, grades and English test score must meet them. If you do not qualify for admission, you cannot receive the scholarship.

### 2. Book your English test early

Many programmes ask for IELTS or TOEFL. Test dates and results can take time, so check your programme's rules now. Do not assume it will be waived.

### 3. Ask your referees this week

Choose lecturers or supervisors who know your work well. Give them the deadline, your CV and a short note on why you are applying. A good referee needs time to write something specific.

### 4. Write a clear motivation

Explain why this programme, why Delft, and what you plan to do afterwards. Be specific and honest, using your own experiences and projects. The words must be yours. At Merit Leaders we review and coach students on their drafts, but we never write essays for you.

### 5. Submit early and keep proof

Aim to submit several days before 1 December. Portals can be slow near deadlines, and time differences can catch people out. Save a copy of everything you send.

## Is it worth applying if the competition is high?

Yes, if you meet the rules and your grades are strong. The scholarship is rare because it combines tuition and a living contribution. But also apply for other options at the same time, so you have a plan B. For example, the [Mundus MAPP scholarship](https://www.meritleaders.com/blog/mundus-mapp-2027-deadline) closes on the same date for people in public policy.

## Beware of scams

TU Delft will never ask you to pay a fee to receive a scholarship. Use only the official tudelft.nl pages. If someone promises you a "guaranteed" award in return for money, walk away.
